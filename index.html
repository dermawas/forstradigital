Service Lines, One Roof

Forstra Digital is a boutique consulting firm founded by senior practitioners with deep experience across Indonesia's BSFI, fintech, and NGO sectors — the industries driving the country's digital economy.

We bring together two capabilities rarely found under one roof: the operational discipline to deliver complex projects on time and within scope, and the strategic clarity to define what should be built — and why.

Beyond our founding team, we work with a curated network of senior professionals across digital, product, and technology. When you engage us, we match the right specialists to your specific challenge — so you always get relevant expertise, not generalist advice.

We work with startups navigating their growth phase and SMEs ready to modernize — businesses that need real expertise, not theory.

What We Do

Two service lines. Rooted in delivery.

Both lines are rooted in what we have actually delivered across banking, fintech, and digital transformation projects in Indonesia — not frameworks learned in a classroom.

01 / Project Management & Delivery

Turn ideas into shipped, working outcomes.

For businesses that have ideas, roadmaps, or backlogs — but struggle to turn them into delivered, working outcomes. We bring structure, governance, and hands-on delivery oversight.

  • End-to-end project management from concept through to delivery
  • PMO setup: governance frameworks, standardized processes, resource planning
  • Agile & Scrum facilitation — sprint planning, backlog grooming, retrospectives
  • Waterfall and hybrid delivery for regulated or compliance-heavy environments
  • Project health checks and recovery for at-risk or stalled initiatives
  • Tooling setup: Jira, Confluence, MS Project, and collaboration platforms
Experience: Digital banking · Core banking rollouts · Telco infrastructure
02 / Digital Strategy & Product Consulting

Build the thinking before the building.

For businesses that know they need to go digital — but are unsure where to start, what to prioritize, or how to make a compelling case to stakeholders.

  • Digital transformation roadmaps tailored to your business context and budget
  • Product strategy: concept development, market positioning, go-to-market planning
  • Business case and proposal development for digital initiatives
  • AI and GenAI application assessment — where it fits, where it doesn't
  • Customer experience and digital channel strategy
  • Technology vendor selection and evaluation support
Experience: Fintech product · Digital banking · Insurance platforms · NGO LMS
